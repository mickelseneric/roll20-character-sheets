Special Attacks

Burst Fire
  • Characters using SMGs may declare burst fire rather than a single shot
  • A burst does the same damage as a single shot
  • If the shot is successful all characters in the ring of adjacent hexes to the target also sustain 1D6 of damage
  • Burst Fire fires 10 shots of ammo
Aimed Shot
  • Target a specific part of an opponent's body
  • Character may move no faster than one-half his Walking MP
  • Targeted location must be unobstructed by cover
  • A successful aimed shot always hits the targeted location
Careful Aim
  • Each round of Careful Aim reduces the To-Hit Target Number by 1 (to a maximum of -3)
  • Characters taking Careful Aim may not move and must have target within their LOS
  • Interrupting Careful Aim (firing at a different target, target moves out of LOS) causes all Careful Aim bonuses to be lost

Special Attacks

Aimed Shots
  • Attacker must have moved no more than one half his MP allowance
  • A successful Aimed Shot always hits the targeted location
Coshing
  • Attackers using Cudgels, Blackjacks, or Clubs may make a special attack from behind, in an attempt to knock their opponent unconscious
  • Requires either a Hit Location Roll of Head or an Aimed Shot to the head
  • A successful hit gives half the weapon's normal damage
  • On a successful hit the target must make a BODY Saving Roll with a penalty of +1 for every level of the attacker's Brawling skill
  • If the Saving Roll fails, the victim becomes unconscious for 1D6 melee rounds per damage point inflicted
All-Out Attacks
  • All-out attacks receive a +2 bonus to the To-Hit Roll
  • Melee attacks against the character performing an All-Out attack also receive a +2 modifier to their To-Hit Roll
Grapple/Tackle
  • Target takes Brawling damage as normal, plus +1 bonus to damage for each two hexes traveled (rounding down)
  • Attacker sustains damage equal to one-half the defender's BODY score (round up)
  • All damage to both Target and Attacker is applied to the torso
  • On a successful attack the defender must make a DEX-based Saving Roll with modifier of Attacker's BODY score - Defender's BODY score to stay standing
  • After a successful attack the attacker maintains control of the defender on successive turns, until the defender escapes. To escape the defender spends an entire combat round and makes a successful DEX-based Saving Roll with the same BODY modifications applied as during the initial Grapple/Tackle attack. There is an additional +3 penalty if the combatants have fallen to the ground
Shields
  • Characters carrying one-handed melee weapons or slug-throwers may carry a small shield in the form of a buckler or Roman round shield to help in their defense
  • Use of a shield adds a +1 modifier to the wearer's own To-Hit Target with weapons, and reduces by one-quarter his MP allowance for any maneuver (i.e., Walking = 3 MP, Dodging/Evading = 5 MP, Running = 7 MP)
  • Attacking - As in a Grappling attack, the difference in BODY is used as a To-Hit Target modifier, and a successful attack requires the defender to make a DEX-based Saving Roll (including the BODY difference) to remain upright. If the defender is able to remain standing, he will be forced into one of his three rear hexes by the attack, and the attacker will advance into the hex vacated by the defender
Firing Ranged Weapons Into Melee
  • Whenever more than one target, friendly or enemy, is present in a hex, the actual recipient of the ranged attack is determined AT RANDOM immediately prior to the To-Hit Roll
  • If the To-Hit Roll fails, the attacker misses all targets in the hex
Martial Arts
  • Characters with Brawling level 4 or more qualify for martial arts skill. They are now able to cosh without a weapon. Their Grapple/Tackle bonus is (BODY+DEX x 2) instead of +1 per every 2 hexes run. They only sustain one-fourth normal damage as a defender or attacker in a Grapple/Tackle attack, and no DEX Saving Roll is required to remain standing after such an attack
Resolving Melee Combat
  • On a successful attack, non-lethal damage equal to the attacker's BODY is applied
  • Damage amounts may be modified by certain skills levels
  • The attacker rolls for hit location, just as with ranged attacks
  • If a critical hit is rolled the specific, lethal damage is applied
  • Consciousness Rolls are made in the same manner as described for lethal HTK
